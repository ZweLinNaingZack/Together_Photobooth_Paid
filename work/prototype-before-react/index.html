A FEW LITTLE ANSWERS

Wondering
about something?

Before you step into the booth.

How does Together work?+

The idea is simple: open a booth, send your person a link, and take photos together from two webcams. Then choose your filters, personalize the strip, and save it. Right now, you can take solo camera photos or try sample photos. Shared rooms with a friend are coming next.

Are my photos and data safe?+

Camera access only starts when you choose “Turn camera on.” Photos, captions, and edits stay in this browser tab; this app does not upload them. Your photocard is created on your device when you download or print it. Refreshing clears your edits. The site host and font provider may receive normal web-request information. We’ll explain live-session privacy before that feature launches.

Do I need to sign up or pay?+

No account or payment is required to try this preview or download a sample strip. Together is a student portfolio project, with a free, no-signup photobooth as the goal.

Can I use it on my phone?+

Yes, this preview adapts to phones, tablets, and desktops. Tap the photo strips to separate them, and tap again to bring them back. Camera access depends on your browser and permission settings. You can also use sample photos to try the flow.

Can I save or print my photocard?+

Yes. Choose PNG or JPG in the booth, then save your sample strip. You can keep the file, share it yourself, or print it. Your download includes the photos from your session, in the layout and style you chose.

A SMALL IDEA, FOR THE PEOPLE YOU MISS

Miles apart.
Still together.

For the dates that happen through a screen.
The best friends in different time zones.
The people you wish were a little closer.

✳

A photobooth without
the same postcode.

Some of the nicest memories are the unplanned ones: a silly face, a laugh you couldn’t hold in, one more photo before you go. Distance shouldn’t mean missing out on those little rituals.

Together is an online photobooth being built for couples and friends who can’t step into a booth side by side. The plan is to bring two webcams into one shared moment, then turn that moment into a photocard you can make your own.

01

Made for your people.

A shared link, a little time together, and room to be yourselves. No profiles to build or feeds to scroll.

02

A keepsake that feels like you.

Pick a mood, choose a frame color, and add a few words. A small souvenir of being together.

03

Built with a little curiosity.

This is a student portfolio project, created while learning computer science and software engineering with AI assistance.

WHERE WE ARE TODAY

A little work in progress.

You can explore the booth with sample photos, try filters and frames, and download a sample strip. Solo webcam capture is available; shared sessions and invitation links are the next chapter.

A LITTLE HELLO BEFORE WE BEGIN

Make yourself
right at home.

A few little steps. A memory to keep.

  1. 01
    Pick your photocard.

    Choose from eight 2 × 6 and 4 × 6-inch layouts, including landscape cards. Each layout sets the number of photos. Next, pick a design on its own page before starting the camera.

  2. 02
    Get in frame.

    Use your camera for a solo session, or try sample photos. Choose manual capture or a 3, 5, or 7-second timer.

  3. 03
    Make it yours.

    Reorder your photos or retake just one. Add a filter if you like, then download or print your chosen photocard.

Shared sessions with a friend are coming next. Camera access is optional and only starts when you turn it on.
