# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-10-14

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** An online photobooth for long-distance couples and friends — snap together live from two places and keep a photocard.
**What it does:** Pick Solo or Duo, choose a layout, and take photos (Duo = both cameras live over a shared link) or upload favourites. Add a frame and filter, then download or print a photocard. Works in the browser on phone or computer; photos never touch our servers.
**Product category:** Online photobooth / photo strip maker ("Korean photobooth online", "4-cut photo", "LDR photobooth").
**Product type:** Consumer web app (B2C), pay-per-session.
**Business model:** 1 free session per account; then 100 points per session. 7,000 MMK = 100 points via KBZPay (manual approval). Only the Duo host pays. Points never expire, non-refundable.

## Target Audience
**Target users:** 16–30, Myanmar first (Burmese UI, KBZPay), Vietnam second (Vietnamese UI). Phone-first, TikTok-native.
**Decision-makers:** The person who starts the booth (host) — usually the more "planner" half of the couple/friendship.
**Primary use case:** Making a shared photo memory with someone who isn't physically there.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Make a long-distance date feel like a real date ("we did something together")
- Get a cute photo strip like a Korean self-photo studio without going to one together
- Mark an occasion — anniversary, birthday, monthsary, "first photo together" before meeting
**Use cases:**
- LDR couples (migrant workers abroad — Thailand, Singapore, Japan, Korea — and partners at home)
- Best friends / siblings living in different cities or countries
- Solo users making a cute strip for their profile or to send someone

## Personas
B2C — single persona: the long-distance partner/friend who wants shared rituals, not just video calls.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** You can video call, but you can't *do* things together — and you have no photos of the two of you.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Video-call screenshots look bad and don't feel like a keepsake
- Physical photobooths (Life4Cuts-style studios) need both people in the same place
- Collage apps (Canva, CapCut templates) are solo work, not a shared moment
**What it costs them:** Missing the "couple photos" everyone else posts; feeling left out of trends.
**Emotional tension:** Missing someone; wanting proof the relationship is "real" and shared.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other online photobooth sites — mostly solo-only, English-only, no live two-camera mode.
**Secondary:** Korean-style self-photo studios — great output but require being together in person.
**Indirect:** Video-call screenshots + collage apps — free but no shared moment, no nice frames.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Duo: two live cameras in one booth from anywhere
- Burmese and Vietnamese UI; local payment (KBZPay)
- Photos stay on your device (privacy)
- Works in the browser — no app install
**Why customers choose us:** It's the only easy way to get a "together" photo strip when you're apart.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Why pay? I can screenshot a call." | First session is free — compare the card to a screenshot yourself. |
| "Is my photo safe?" | Photos never go to our servers; Duo photos go straight between your two devices. |
| "Is it hard / will the camera work?" | No app; open the link, allow camera. Telegram support if stuck. |
| "Paying by transfer feels risky." | Small amount, admin-approved, receipts kept; Telegram support. |

**Anti-persona:** People wanting free unlimited edits; pro photographers; event-booth businesses.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Bad call screenshots; no couple photos to post.
**Pull:** Cute strip, live "together" moment, free first try.
**Habit:** Video calls + screenshots are "good enough" and free.
**Anxiety:** Paying a new site by bank transfer; camera/connection not working.
